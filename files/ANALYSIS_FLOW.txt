BORD ANALYSIS: GAME FLOW, FEEDBACK, PLAYER UNDERSTANDING
==========================================================

1. BUREAU MANUAL PROBLEMS
- Never properly taught. Intro says "Consult" but never says press TAB
- Tab on case file has no visual emphasis - looks identical to "Case File" tab
- Manual describes rules but NEVER teaches the click mechanic
- First loophole (Okonkwo date) requires cross-referencing 2D UI with 3D desk paper
  The directive date is ONLY on the 3D memo, not in the manual. Hardest loophole first.

2. LOOPHOLE DIFFICULTY RATINGS
  Case 2  (Okonkwo date):    HARD   - cross-media reference, 3-step inference
  Case 4  (Brandt suffix):   MEDIUM - regulation SEC-1 is clear
  Case 6  (Wojcik janitor):  EASY   - most discoverable
  Case 9  (Osei signature):  MEDIUM - clear logic, slightly unintuitive click target
  Case 12 (Kader offenses):  MEDIUM - [MISSING] tags are visible
  Case 14 (Your file suffix): MEDIUM - emotional distraction factor

3. DIRECTIVE LABEL PROBLEM
  "REVENUE ENFORCEMENT" sounds like an order to PUNISH
  Player reads "enforce" and thinks they should condemn
  But regulations under it actually enable mercy
  Directive names steer toward evil - opposite of mechanical purpose

4. FEEDBACK AFTER CHOICES
  Mercy: ominous (lamp dims, typing stops, "Noted.") - makes mercy FEEL dangerous
  Evil: cold approval ("Your compliance is noted.") - makes evil feel safe
  Correct loophole: ONLY unambiguous positive feedback (green VOID, warm chord)
  Wrong loophole: clear negative (red flash, dissonant buzz, +15 suspicion)

  Player cannot tell if mercy vs evil is "right" - this is intentional
  But the ambiguity means first-time players have no idea what's happening

5. SUSPICION IS INVISIBLE
  Player never sees a number or meter
  Evil REDUCES suspicion (counterintuitive - player assumes evil = dangerous)
  Threshold messages help but are brief (2.5s) and easy to miss mid-case
  Player who never presses SPACE misses most suspicion feedback
  Look hint is nearly invisible: 11px, 25% opacity white

6. PACING
  Shift 1 (5 cases): Good escalation, alternates loophole/non-loophole
  Shift 2 (6 cases): Slight stall - Cases 9-10 dip after Henrik Voss reveal
  Shift 3 (7 cases): Longest shift, fatigue risk. Cases 16-17 feel thin

7. NEWSPAPERS
  Good reactive storytelling - 3 of 5 Shift 1 cases get coverage
  Evil headlines use sanitized Bureau language ("smooth transition for detained minors")
  Missing: Brandt, Dr. Nasir (Shift 1), Wojcik, Osei, Dimitri (Shift 2) get no coverage
  No newspaper after Shift 3 - most impactful choices get no newspaper feedback
